Backend Override // Priority Channel

One backend.
Every hub.
Total command.

The control desk can overrule every tile at once. Trigger the emergency corridor and the whole city goes red.

Each hub runs its own sensor logic until the backend speaks. An override is pushed down and obeyed the next time a tile polls. This reverses the data flow: hubs stop reporting up, the brain starts commanding down.
